ORIGIN STORY

Hi, I'm Lily.

I'm a creative focused on advertising, internet culture, visual storytelling and the social spark found in pubs and footy grounds.

With an art alias called Rat Mustard, I've been part of Australia's creative scene for over eight years, working across galleries, podcasts, film, music projects and DJ sets.

Advertising felt like a natural next step — combining creativity, culture, strategy and human behaviour.
